About Us

Building Every Student’s Potential

Prime Group of Institutions aims to understand every student’s individual strengths, develop their talents, and connect them with the right opportunities to grow.

Our Vision

Education that recognises every talent

To create a student-centred education system that regularly identifies each learner’s strengths through daily, weekly, monthly, quarterly and yearly assessments. We aim to nurture those talents and connect every student with suitable platforms, opportunities and placements.

Our Mission

Turning potential into progress

  • Assess students consistently and understand their individual talents, interests and development needs.
  • Provide personalised guidance, practical learning and engaging opportunities to strengthen each student’s abilities.
  • Connect students with suitable platforms, real-world experiences and placement opportunities to help them achieve their goals.
Why Choose Prime Group?

A pathway for every learner

  • Student-focused learning that recognises individual strengths.
  • Continuous assessment and talent development, not just one-time evaluation.
  • Guidance and opportunities designed to help students apply their skills and move towards their future goals.

FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

How many students are in a batch?

Batch size depends on the programme. Please enquire for current batch details.

Is there a demo class?

Yes. Book a demo class for just ₹99 using “Book Demo at ₹99” and pick a slot within the next 3 days.

What are the fees?

Fees depend on the course and class. Enquire and we will share the full details.

Are classes online or offline?

Prime Digit offers online classes. Other groups run at our branches.

Where can I see results?

Verified results are shown in the Results & Achievements section on each group page.

Which groups are open right now?

Prime Institute, Prime Move and Grow and Prime Digit are open. Prime Skill Institute and Prime Sport Academy are launching soon — use “Notify me” to be told when they open.

How do I enquire or join?

Tap “Enquire Now” to send your details, or book a demo class for just ₹99 first. Our team will get in touch with you.
